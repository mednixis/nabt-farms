Our Story

Rooted in the land,
grown for the future.

Nabt Farms was built on one conviction: that Egyptian soil, nurtured with precision and respect, produces some of the finest food in the world. Using hydroponic and sandponic growing systems, we produce year-round with zero pesticides and precise nutrient control — delivering consistently premium quality to restaurants, retailers, and supermarkets across Egypt.

How We Work

Precision at
every stage.

I
Soil & Water Testing

Every growth cycle begins with rigorous analysis of growing media and nutrient solution to ensure peak conditions from day one.

II
Hydroponic & Sandponic Systems

Our hydroponic and sandponic growing systems use automated nutrient delivery and real-time crop monitoring — producing cleaner, faster, and more consistent yields than traditional soil farming.

III
Harvest & Grading

Produce is hand-selected at peak maturity, then graded and tested before leaving the farm — every batch, every time.

IV
Cold Chain Delivery

Temperature-controlled logistics from farm gate to your facility — same-day delivery across Cairo and greater Egypt.
