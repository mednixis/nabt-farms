Crop 02 · Batavia lettuce

Batavia lettuce, crisp all year.

Batavia sits between a crisphead and a loose leaf: dense enough to hold structure under dressing, open enough to keep a clean, sweet bite with none of the bitterness that comes from heat-stressed field lettuce.

Because we control light, nutrient and temperature, the heads that leave in August look like the heads that leave in February. That is the whole point of growing indoors, and it is what a kitchen actually buys.

How we grow

Precision at every stage.

Four steps, every cycle, every crop. Nothing here is left to the weather.

I

Water and nutrient testing

Every cycle opens with analysis of the nutrient solution and growing media, so conditions are right from day one rather than corrected on day ten.

II

Controlled growing

Hydroponic and sandponic systems with automated nutrient delivery and continuous monitoring — cleaner, faster and more consistent than soil.

III

Harvest and grading

Hand-cut at peak maturity, then graded before anything leaves the farm. Every batch, every time.

IV

Cold chain delivery

Temperature-controlled from farm gate to your door, same day across Cairo and greater Egypt.

Standards

Growing with the planet, not against it.

Zero pesticides

Closed growing systems mean there is nothing to spray, and no chemical residue to wash off.

Water stewardship

Closed-loop drip irrigation recirculates the nutrient solution instead of losing it to the ground.

Solar powered

On-site solar carries the growing rooms and cold storage through the day.

Precision monitoring

Sensors track EC, pH, temperature and humidity continuously, so problems surface before they reach a tray.

Graded every batch

Nothing ships that we would not put on our own pass. Rejected trays go to compost, not to customers.
